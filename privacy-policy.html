Privacy Policy

Prospere — Self-Improvement App

This Privacy Policy describes how DubayaApps (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Prospere mobile application (“the App”). By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

1. The Short Version

What you write in Prospere stays on your phone. Your Inner Work answers, goals, activities, wins, vision board, and Annual Reflections are stored on your device, not on our servers. We keep only what’s needed to run your account, and we never sell your information or use it for advertising.

2. What Stays on Your Phone

Everything you create in the App — your Inner Work answers, goals and activities across every timeframe, the activities you tick off, your Win Box, your vision board photos and phrases, the Annual Reflection PDFs in your Reflection Library, and your reminder settings — is stored only on your device. We cannot see it, and it is not backed up by us. That is why the App lets you make your own backup (Section 6).

3. What We Keep on Our Servers

  • Your account: email address, first and last name, and your password (stored as a secure hash — we never see it).
  • Your profile photo, if you add one. It is stored at a web address that is not listed anywhere, but anyone who has that exact address can view it.
  • Your time zone and reminder level, plus the date you completed Inner Work, so we can send the one-time email reminder described in Section 5 at the right time — or not at all if you’ve chosen Silent.
  • A count of AI-writing requests (just the time of each, no content), used only to apply fair-use limits. These records are deleted after two days.

4. AI Writing Help

Users have the option to use AI in some areas of this app. When elected, data is sent without your name or email. We don’t keep what is sent, and your data will not be used to train AI models.

5. Notifications and Email

Reminders are scheduled entirely on your phone. Nothing is sent to our servers to deliver them, and you can change their frequency or turn them off in Settings. The one exception is a single email, about a year after you complete Inner Work, inviting you to revisit it. It is sent through our email provider, Resend, using only your email address and first name.

6. Backups

You can save a backup from Settings → Backup & Restore. It is a single file, locked with a password you choose, that you save wherever you like — your email, your files, or your own cloud storage. We never receive it and never see your password, which also means we cannot recover a backup if the password is forgotten.

7. Service Providers

  • Supabase (United States) — hosts the account information and files described in Section 3. supabase.com/security
  • Anthropic — AI writing help (Section 4). anthropic.com/legal/privacy
  • Resend — sends the one-year reminder email (Section 5).
  • Sentry — if the App hits an unexpected error, a crash report may be sent so we can fix it. It includes your device model, system and app version, and technical details of the error — never your answers, goals, or other content. sentry.io/privacy

We use no advertising or analytics services, and we do not collect your location, contacts, or health data. The App asks for access to your photos only to add pictures you choose, and to your calendar only to add an activity you choose to add.

8. Your Choices and Deleting Your Account

You can update your name, email address, and profile photo at any time in Settings.

To delete your account, go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. Your account and profile photo are deleted immediately, along with everything stored for your account on that phone, including your Reflection Library. If you would like a copy first, save a backup before deleting. If you can no longer sign in, email Prospere@dubayaapps.com from your account’s email address and we will delete it within 30 days.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California, you may have additional rights to access, correct, or port your personal data. Contact us to exercise them.

9. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy we will update the “Effective Date” below. Continuing to use the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
